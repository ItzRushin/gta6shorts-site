Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who uses the App

"GTA6 Shorts" (the "App") is used only by its owner, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up.

What data the App accesses

  • The owner's own account authorization (OAuth access tokens from YouTube and TikTok), used only to upload approved videos. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and never shared.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's TikTok account (TikTok scope user.info.basic), required by TikTok's login.

The App does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people, and does not access any other user's account.

Public stream content

Clips come from public live streams. Streamers are credited in each video. A creator who wants a clip removed can contact us through the channel and it will be taken down.

Third parties

Uploads go through the official APIs of Google/YouTube and TikTok, under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the YouTube or TikTok account settings.

Contact

Through the GTA 6 Daily Momentz YouTube or TikTok channel.
